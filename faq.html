Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for stays, dining, events, transfers and booking.

How do I book a room?

Use the Book a stay button, select your dates and room, then submit your guest details. Live availability and payment will be connected when the backend is enabled.

Can I request an airport transfer?

Yes. Use the contact page and include your flight details, arrival time and number of passengers.

Can I book a meeting or event?

Yes. Send the event date, guest count, room setup and catering requirements through the meetings page.

Can I reserve a table at TARO?

Yes. Use the dining reservation form. The live reservation system can be connected to the same backend later.

Will my card be charged on this website?

The current front-end package does not process payments. A secure payment gateway should be added only after the hotel’s Supabase/backend setup is completed.
